# Questions for Secure64 before this goes live

Things found while reading the whole of secure64.com on 2026-11-01. Useful in the pitch, since most of them are small problems on the current site that the rebuild would clear up.

## Conflicts on the current site

| Topic | What the site says | What we did |
|---|---|---|
| Support hours | Homepage: "24/7/365 support staff". Support page: phone support is "during business hours according to contract SLAs"; the support website is 7 x 24 | Used the support page wording. No "24/7" on the phone number or Under Attack page |
| Filter categories | LineGUARD: "over 150". LineGUARD Edge: "over 57" | Used 150+ on LineGUARD and left the number off Edge |
| DNS Manager or DNS Director | ProVision and DDI pages mention "DNS Director". The product list has DNS Manager | Used DNS Manager and left Director out |
| Vizion or Vision | DNS Cache page uses both | Used Vizion |
| Product names | LineGUARD/LineGuard, DNS GUARD/DNS Guard, ProVision/Pro Vision | Used LineGUARD, DNS GUARD, ProVision |
| Ian Sampson's title | Compliance Officer (team page). Chief Marketing Officer (Oct 2025 release) | Used Compliance Officer |
| Hilco | "Hilco Digital" on the ProVision page, "Hilco Global" in the release | Used "Hilco" or "Hilco Global" |
| DHCP | DDI FAQ says no standalone DHCP product; DDI overview treats DHCP as core | Described ProVision as the DHCP layer |

## Bugs on the current site

- Email links point to a staging domain (`secure641stg.wpenginepowered.com`), including the support address. We show no email addresses until the real ones are confirmed.
- The ENUM subtitle is repeated on the Deploying DNSSEC and Transitioning to IPv6 pages.
- Typos: "depoyments", "fules-based", "DCHP", "Cisco Umbrellaplatform".
- Cookie banner placeholders reported on first read: `{title}` and `{vendor_count}`.
- Services, News and Company menu items have no landing page.

## Claims left out until they can be backed up

- "Most secure DNSSEC solution on Earth" and "the only fully automated DNSSEC signing solution".
- "Immune to malware".
- "99.999% uptime" for ENUM: no basis given.
- The LineGUARD comparison table against unnamed "Competitor Solutions".
- "Predictable pricing": no pricing model is published.
- The "Secure64 AI" homepage block: too vague to describe. What does it do?
- "One of every three home users" infected: no source given.

## Figures used, which need a source Secure64 can stand behind

- 6 million users, 100 million threats a day, 50% lower cost, 3x performance, under six weeks. These appear on the Solutions page and in the Oct 2024 release. Can the carrier be named?
- 400k QPS in virtual environments: test conditions aren't stated.
- More than 95% of cyberthreats have a DNS element: no source given.
- 1.5 billion users: the latest milestone we can date is 1 billion in Feb 2019.

## What we need from Secure64

- Logo files, and approval to show customer logos. The customer list and partner list were read from logo images on the current site, so please confirm them. Cuallis and Grupo CESA appeared only in file names and were left out.
- Real photography of the team and the Fort Collins office. Nothing on this site is stock, and real photos would help more than any other single change.
- Result numbers for the four case studies. We couldn't extract text from the PDFs, so the summaries come from the listing page. Could any customer be named?
- A customer quote with permission. The March 2025 blog post has one from a government agency.
- What the Try Secure64 offer includes (length, eligibility). The current page is only a form.
- What happens after someone uses the Under Attack form, and whether phone cover is really available out of hours.
- Is CloudDNS (2023), OneGuard (2021) or TotalGuard (2019) still sold? None is in the product list.
- Forms: the current Zoho forms can be embedded here. Until then, buttons link to secure64.com.
